Weekly Measurement

Sukatin ang tama, hindi ang marami

📊 Cadence & Output

  • Zero zero-activity weeks (prior audit found 24%)
  • Actual posts vs. 35-slot template, weekly
  • Every slot published inside one of the 4 windows

🎯 Audience Quality

  • % of engagement from corrected-territory geos vs. outside (once geo-fencing is live)
  • Reactions-to-impressions ratio — flag any post above 25% for manual review
  • Net follows correlated to post days, not silent spikes

🎬 Retention & Response

  • Avg seconds viewed on Reels (target ≥ 30s of a ≤30–45s Reel)
  • Comments answered within 24h — Messenger auto-reply on
  • Persona CTAs: volunteer signups, Women's Wing joins, dialogue RSVPs, household registrations
