# PARTIDO BANGON BANGSAMORO (PBB)
## User Personas for the 2026 BARMM Parliamentary Elections
### Derived from the Political-Economic Landscape Report

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

These six voter personas represent the core constituencies PBB must mobilize to secure proportional representation seats in the September 14, 2026 BARMM Parliamentary Elections. Each persona is grounded in the economic, political, and social data from the Landscape Report — including the ₱1,790 daily wage gap, the 71,000+ member base, the four currents of BARMM politics, and PBB's own three-segment communications strategy.

**Primary Personas (Voter-Facing):**
1. The Aspiring Professional (Youth Engine + Professional Class)
2. The Struggling Provider (Laylayan / Margins)
3. The Community Matriarch (Women & Gender Empowerment)
4. The Peace Advocate (Former Combatants & Conflict-Affected Communities)
5. The Business Builder (Halal & Green Economy)
6. The Concerned Elder (Traditional & Faith-Based Leaders)

**Secondary Personas (Internal / Support):**
7. The PBB Ground Operator (Officials & Nominees)
8. The Strategic Ally (Donors & Professional Associations)

- **Put food on the table every day** without the ₱1,790 daily shortfall
- **Access free healthcare** for his children and elderly parents

- **Send his children to school** without dropping out due to poverty
- **Feel safe** during elections (his municipality may be "Red" or "Orange")

### Pain Points & Frustrations
- The ₱411 minimum wage vs. ₱2,201 Family Living Wage is crushing
- No local health center with free lab tests or medicines; Super Health Stations don't exist yet
- Children are stunted due to poor nutrition — a lifetime handicap
- Politicians visit during elections with empty promises, then disappear
- Fear of election violence in "Red" zones (Lanao del Sur, Maguindanao del Sur)
