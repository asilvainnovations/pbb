Who we are

Powered by youth and professionals.

We are mobilizing the young doctors, lawyers, engineers, and educators of the BARMM — the generation that grew up inside the peace process and is ready to build what comes after it.

Our theory of change

Educated youth and civil society are our electoral power.

Every volunteer, every barangay conversation, every young professional who signs up feeds a single current: a Bangsamoro parliament that answers to its people, not to patronage.

Demand for meritocracy

Rejecting jobs secured by clan connections in favor of skill-based opportunity — in government and in our own candidate list.

Demand for transparency

Prioritizing anti-corruption safeguards and participatory governance in the new parliamentary system, from budgeting to hiring.

Our platform of government

The BANGON Platform

Six letters. Six commitments. This is what a BANGON-led Bangsamoro government delivers, in plain terms — no jargon, no fine print.

BANGON
B Basic Services Enhancement

Services that reach every household
  • Free internet access in schools
  • Super Health Stations with free lab work and medicine
  • ₱8,000 subsidy for Bangsamoro households
A Accelerate Sustainable Livelihood

Jobs and income, close to home
  • Negosyo centers in every municipality
  • Socio-economic reintegration packages for former combatants
  • Direct support for coastal communities
N Non-violent Peace

Peace built by dialogue, not force
  • Community-based dialogues to prevent and resolve conflict
  • Formal mediation mechanisms for rido and clan disputes
  • Interfaith unity programs across Bangsamoro, IP, and settler communities
G Green Economic Competitiveness

Growth that protects the land and sea
  • Direct subsidies for farmers and fisherfolk
  • Eco-farming initiatives and climate-smart agriculture
  • Incentives to attract clean, sustainable investment
O Open & Inclusive Governance

A government you can see into
  • Merit-based, decentralized hiring — no more padrino system
  • Open budgeting the public can review line by line
  • Digitalization of government services and permits
N Nurturing Women & Gender Empowerment

Women lead, protected and equipped
  • Stronger protection against gender-based violence
  • Dedicated women's entrepreneurship programs
  • Expanded maternal health services

How BANGON becomes law

The 5-Year Legislative Architecture

A sequenced, executable plan rooted in the Bangsamoro Organic Law — feasible through public policy, public-private partnerships, and technical know-how. Three wings turn the BANGON platform into filed bills.

Wing A

Foundational Services & Livelihood

  • Establishing the BARMM ECCD Inter-Agency Council
  • Super Health Stations Act
  • Magna Carta for the Poor
  • Zakat System Regulation Act

Wing B — Ecological Green

Green Governance & Economic Competitiveness

  • Bangsamoro Investments and Incentives Code
  • Environment & Natural Resources Code
  • Climate Change Commission Act
  • Ligawasan Development Authority Act

Wing C — PBB Gold

Peace, Inclusion & Open Governance

  • Marawi Siege Victims Compensation Act
  • Bottom-Up Budgeting Institutionalization
  • Bangsamoro Gender and Development Code

The 2026 roadmap

Spanning the transition, one horizon at a time

From grassroots organizing today, to a legislative blueprint before election day, to seats in parliament this September.

Horizon 1 · Current

The Foundation

Grassroots and sectoral organizing. Activating our youth and professional base, and standing up headquarters and strategic partnerships across BARMM.

Horizon 2 · Pre-Election

The Blueprint

Framing legislative action through the Bangsamoro Organic Law — executing mandates for skills training, science & technology, and Halal standardization in public discourse.

Horizon 3 · September 2026

The Victory

Securing parliamentary seats — District and Party representatives — to enact the reformist BANGON agenda from inside the Bangsamoro Parliament.

Campaign tactics

Participatory democracy, not patronage

PBB's strategy for 2026 breaks from top-down political mobilization. We are transferring actual decision-making power to the next generation.

What we're replacing

Top-down machinery

  • A single patron directing votes downward through loyal intermediaries
  • Rallies built on obligation, not conviction
  • Decisions made for communities, not with them

What we're building

A grassroots network

  • Sectoral organizing — professional forums, town halls, and feedback loops replacing patron-client rallies
  • Youth in power — young professionals fielded for contested District and Party seats
  • Decision-making power transferred, not just promised

Signature programs

Where the BANGON platform gets its edge

Three flagship programs that turn our six pillars into technical, deliverable engineering — not slogans.

The Modernity Bridge

Overhauling TESDA-BARMM to align vocational training with emerging industries, and fostering advanced innovation through regional scholarships and research councils.

The Halal Economy Ecosystem

Global tourism, Shari'ah-compliant Islamic finance, a specialized certified workforce, and scientific testing integrity — Halal treated as a multi-billion-dollar global market, not just a local baseline.

Ecological Justice & Green Skills

Renewable energy engineering, sustainable agriculture, and climate-resilient infrastructure — the only regional platform that treats climate action as a prerequisite for economic growth.
